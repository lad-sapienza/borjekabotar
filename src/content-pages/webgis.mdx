import { Map } from "@lad-sapienza/scms-core/components/Map";

The map below shows the pigeon towers documented in the province of Isfahan
during the 2008 survey — mainly along the oasis of Isfahan, crossed by the
Zayandè river, and around Golpayegan. Use the layer control to switch between
the street map and satellite imagery.
